Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Information we collect

If you contact us or submit a booking enquiry, we may collect the details you provide, such as your name, contact information, address and information about the garden service you are requesting.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, discuss and arrange services, and communicate with you about your request.

Sharing and handling information

We do not publish your enquiry details. We may use website hosting or form-processing providers to operate this site and handle submissions. Those providers may process information on our behalf. Check your booking form provider’s privacy information for details about its handling of submissions.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we receive. No method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and questions

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have sent us, contact gregsgardenrescue@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
